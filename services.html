Service 01

How Our VAPT Process Works

Our VAPT process simulates real-world cyberattacks to identify security weaknesses in your web applications, APIs, servers, and network infrastructure. We provide clear reports and practical recommendations to help you fix every issue.

1. Security Assessment

We perform automated scans to identify common vulnerabilities, outdated software, and security misconfigurations.

2. Manual Penetration Testing

Our security experts manually test your systems to uncover vulnerabilities that automated tools often miss.

3. Detailed Security Report

Receive an easy-to-understand report with risk levels, technical details, and practical recommendations for remediation.

4. Re-Testing

Receive an easy-to-understand report with risk levels, technical details, and practical recommendations for remediation.

Methodology

Our 5-Step Security Assessment Process

Disciplined, transparent, and structured testing lifecycle.

1

Scope

Define targets, boundaries, IP whitelisting, and rules of engagement.

2

Planning

We define the scope, identify systems to be tested, and agree on the assessment process.

3

Security Testing

We perform automated scans and manual penetration testing to identify vulnerabilities.

4

Reporting

A detailed report explains every finding, its impact, and how to fix it.

5

Remediation

Our experts help your development or IT team understand and resolve security issues.
