Service 01

VAPT Service Breakdown

Our Vulnerability Assessment and Penetration Testing evaluates your digital assets from an attacker's perspective. We systematically test web applications, REST APIs, and server infrastructure.

1. Baseline Scanning

Automated auditing to catch unpatched CVEs, SSL errors, and configuration flaws.

2. Deep Manual Testing

Human exploitation targeting authorization bypasses, SQLi, and business logic bugs.

3. Risk Reports

CVSS 3.1 scored findings with step-by-step reproduction scripts for developers.

4. Fix Verification

Patch re-testing to confirm complete resolution before final report sign-off.

Methodology

Our 5-Step VAPT Engagement Methodology

Disciplined, transparent, and structured testing lifecycle.

1

Scope

Define targets, boundaries, IP whitelisting, and rules of engagement.

2

Test

Execute automated baseline scans and deep manual penetration testing.

3

Report

Document CVSS findings, PoC scripts, and remediation guidelines.

4

Remediate

Provide direct engineering guidance to developer teams fixing issues.

5

Re-Test

Re-verify patches to ensure full resolution of all reported vulnerabilities.
