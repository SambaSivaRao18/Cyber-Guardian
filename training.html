Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our cybersecurity training programs, workshops, and enrollment process.

Do students need prior hacking experience for the training?

No. Our training is beginner-friendly and starts with cybersecurity fundamentals before moving to practical ethical hacking and penetration testing exercises.

Who can join this cybersecurity training program?

Anyone interested in cybersecurity can join, including students, recent graduates, IT professionals, and beginners. Basic computer knowledge is helpful but not required.

Can workshops be conducted on-site at colleges?

Yes. We conduct customized workshops and hands-on training sessions at colleges, universities, and educational institutions based on their requirements.

What topics are covered in the training

The program covers cybersecurity fundamentals, networking, Linux basics, web security, ethical hacking, vulnerability assessment, penetration testing, and practical cybersecurity tools.

Is the training completely practical or only theoretical?
