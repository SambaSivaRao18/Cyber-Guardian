Frequently Asked Questions About Training

Find answers to common questions regarding enrollment, prerequisites, and certificates.

Do students need prior hacking experience for the training?

No prior penetration testing experience is required. The curriculum builds systematically from foundational networking concepts up to hands-on exploitation techniques.

Who can join this cybersecurity training program?

The program is suitable for students, fresh graduates, and anyone interested in building a career in cybersecurity. Basic computer knowledge and interest in security are recommended.

Can workshops be conducted on-site at colleges?

Yes. We deliver live on-premise training sessions and hands-on workshops tailored to college schedules across the region.

What topics are covered in the training

The training covers cybersecurity fundamentals, networking basics, Linux essentials, vulnerability assessment, web security, penetration testing concepts, security tools, and real-world attack and defense scenarios.

Is the training completely practical or only theoretical?

The training focuses on hands-on learning. Participants work with cybersecurity tools, practice in controlled lab environments, and perform practical exercises based on real-world security scenarios.
